# Brightpath Technologies: Employee Expense Policy (v1.1)

This policy applies to all Brightpath employees in every office. The company's base currency is INR. All limits below are in INR; claims in other currencies are converted using the rates in Section 13 before limits are checked.

## 1. General Rules
1.1 Every claim must include: claimant, date, category, amount, currency, description, and whether a receipt is available.
1.2 Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date. Older claims require finance review.
1.3 Expense dates cannot be in the future.
1.4 The description must state a clear business purpose.
1.5 Valid categories are: Meals, Client Entertainment, Ground Transport, Air and Rail Travel, Lodging, Office Supplies, Equipment, Software, Training. A claim with an empty or "Other" category must be classified during review.

## 2. Receipts
2.1 A receipt is required for every claim above INR 500 (after currency conversion).
2.2 If a receipt is lost, the claimant must provide a written explanation; the claim will be sent for manual review.

## 3. Meals
3.1 Individual business meals are reimbursable up to INR 1,500 per person per day.
3.2 Team meals require the number of attendees; the per-person limit in 3.1 applies.
3.3 Alcohol is not reimbursable under any category.

## 4. Client Entertainment
4.1 Meals or events with clients or prospects are reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per event.
4.2 The claim must name the client organisation and the attendees.
4.3 Entertainment above INR 5,000 requires prior written approval from a department head.

## 5. Ground Transport
5.1 Taxis, ride-hailing, and auto-rickshaws for business travel are reimbursable up to INR 2,000 per trip.
5.2 Daily commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## 6. Air and Rail Travel
6.1 Economy class only, up to INR 25,000 per trip.
6.2 Bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance where possible.

## 7. Lodging
7.1 Hotel stays are reimbursable up to INR 8,000 per night.
7.2 Room upgrades, minibar, and personal services are not reimbursable.

## 8. Office Supplies and Equipment
8.1 Office supplies (stationery, notebooks, printer paper) are reimbursable up to INR 3,000 per claim.
8.2 Equipment (monitors, chairs, keyboards, headsets, electronics) requires prior IT approval regardless of amount, up to INR 15,000 per claim.

## 9. Software and Subscriptions
9.1 Work-related software subscriptions are reimbursable up to INR 5,000 per month.
9.2 Personal entertainment subscriptions (e.g. streaming services) are not reimbursable.

## 10. Training and Learning
10.1 Courses, certifications, and books related to the employee's role are reimbursable up to INR 20,000 per year.
10.2 Training requires prior manager approval; the claim must reference it.

## 11. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
11.1 Personal items, traffic fines, penalties, alcohol, and gifts to employees are not reimbursable.

## 12. Duplicates and Split Claims
12.1 The same expense must not be submitted more than once.
12.2 Splitting a single expense into multiple claims to stay under a limit is prohibited.

## 13. Currencies and Exchange Rates
13.1 Supported currencies and the fixed rates for this period are: 1 USD = INR 83, 1 EUR = INR 90, 1 GBP = INR 105, 1 INR = INR 1.
13.2 Claims in any other currency cannot be processed automatically and require finance review.
13.3 Totals and limit checks always use the converted INR amount.
